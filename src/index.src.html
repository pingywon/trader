Insider Tracker

A bot that copies what company insiders buy.

When a CEO or director buys their own company's stock, they have to tell the SEC within two business days. This tool reads those reports and places a small matching buy through Alpaca.

  1. WatchReads new insider reports from the SEC.SEC EDGAR, Form 4
  2. DecideKeeps real purchases by top executives. Picks an order size.$100 to $3,000, never over 5% of the account
  3. BuyPlaces the order through Alpaca and logs it.Paper trading, unless you ask twice

Set up for paper trading: practice money, real market prices.

How it talks to Alpaca

  • How much is in the account?GET /v2/account
  • Can this stock be traded?GET /v2/assets/{symbol}
  • Buy this many dollars of it.POST /v2/orders
