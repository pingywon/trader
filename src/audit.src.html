The tool

It reads insider reports from the SEC and places a small matching buy through Alpaca. It only buys. Overview

Order sizes
Insider spentTool orders

$5 million and up$3,000

How it uses Alpaca

Three calls through Alpaca's Python SDK.

CallRequestUses
get_account()GET /v2/accountequity, for the 5% cap
get_asset()GET /v2/assets/{symbol}tradable
submit_order()POST /v2/orderssaves id and status
{"symbol": "FUL", "notional": 800.0, "side": "buy", "type": "market", "time_in_force": "day"}

The order: a dollar amount, at market price, good for the day.

Keys, endpoints and what it never calls
  • Keys: ALPACA_API_KEY and ALPACA_API_SECRET, from the environment or .env.
  • Endpoint: ALPACA_PAPER picks practice or real money.
  • Never calls: positions, open orders, order status, buying power, clock, quotes, cancel, sell.

What held up
  • No keys in the repo or its 10 commits.
  • Paper is the default when the switch is unset.
  • A second run over the same filings sent no orders.
  • The 5% cap cut a $3,000 order to $1,250.
  • Safe SQL and YAML. Nothing shells out.
  • About 4 requests a second to the SEC. The limit is 10.
  • 815 lines, six small modules, a reason logged for every decision.

Fix first

  1. Change the SEC request to owner=only.
  2. Done: live trading now has to be asked for twice.
  3. Check positions and cash before every order.

Method

Read all 815 lines, then ran it: the SEC's current feed, 193 real filings, a local stand-in for Alpaca.
